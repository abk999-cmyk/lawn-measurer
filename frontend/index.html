📍 How to Use

  1. Search for your address using the search box (top-left), or zoom/pan manually
  2. Click the polygon tool to start drawing
  3. Click on the map to draw points around your lawn area
  4. Complete the polygon by clicking the first point again
  5. Click "Analyze Lawn" to process your lawn
